# Code of Conduct

Puddle welcomes contributors of all experience levels and backgrounds. Help make
issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces respectful and useful.

## Community standards

- Be considerate, patient, and clear. Make room for questions and newcomers.
- Discuss the behavior or code, and offer specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect differences in identity, background, ability, and perspective.
- Respect privacy. Share only information you have permission to publish.
- Accept corrections and moderation decisions, and help resolve disagreements.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct,
deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not welcome.
These expectations also apply when someone represents the project in another
public space.

## Reporting a concern

For conduct in this repository, contact the maintainer
[@linson007](https://github.com/linson007). If no private contact method is listed,
you can ask for one in an issue without posting the incident details or anyone's
personal information. A dedicated confidential project reporting channel has not
yet been established.

You can also use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls for
platform-level concerns. Do not repost abusive material or contact someone who
has asked you to stop. Share only the context needed to understand a report.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, request a
change in behavior, or restrict participation to protect the community. They
should consider context and impact, explain decisions when appropriate, and
handle report details with care. Sensitive details should not be made public as
part of an explanation. Report handling and response times are best effort.
